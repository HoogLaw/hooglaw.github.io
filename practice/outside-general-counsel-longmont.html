What outside general counsel means

Outside general counsel is an attorney who serves your business on an ongoing basis without being on your payroll. You get the same relationship you would have with in-house counsel, an attorney who knows your agreements, your structure, your industry, and your goals, without the cost of a full-time hire.

For most small and mid-sized businesses in Longmont and Boulder County, this is the most practical way to have real legal support. You call before signing a contract, not after a problem develops.

What Hoog Law handles as outside general counsel

The scope varies by client, but typically includes contract review and drafting, governance questions, employment agreements, vendor relationships, lease reviews, and advising on business decisions with legal implications. For matters outside the core practice areas, Hoog Law helps coordinate with the right specialist rather than leaving you to find one yourself.

Why it works for growing businesses

The businesses that benefit most from outside general counsel are past the startup stage, generating real revenue, entering into significant contracts, and dealing with legal questions often enough that ad hoc representation is inefficient. Having an attorney who already knows your documents and your situation saves time and produces better advice than starting from scratch each time something comes up.

The most expensive legal problems are usually the ones that could have been avoided with a five-minute conversation before a decision was made. Outside general counsel changes that equation.
